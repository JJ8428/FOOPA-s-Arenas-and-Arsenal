------- FOOPA's Arenas and Arsenal -------
by FOOPA_luvr

There are plenty of mods that add tools that assist in tasks like mining, painting, placing blocks/walls, and building arenas.
However, these mods never do it in a way that I consider balanced.

This mod adds a variety of tools inspired by the Rubblemaker to address those exact tasks, whilst remaining (in my view at least) balanced and vanilla-esque.

TOOLS & FEATIRES:

1. Automatic Auto-Mattock (V1 & V2)
    - Toggleable area mining pickaxes: V1 (up to 5x5) and V2 (up to 7x7).
    - Speed dynamically normalizes per brush size to prevent broken mining rates.
    - Pickaxe power matches the highest-tier pickaxe found in your Inventory, Piggy Bank, or Safe.
    - Able to mine evil stone to streamline underground boss arena prep on FTW and difficult mods.
2. Another Auto-Hammer (V1 & V2)
    - Multi-tile wall-clearing hammers: V1 (up to 5x5) and V2 (up to 7x7).
    - Power scales with your best inventory/piggy bank/safe hammer.
    - 1x1 mode safely retains the ability to smash Demon Altars, Shadow Orbs, and Crimson Hearts. Sloping behavior moved to dedicated tools.
3. Tile Pounder
    - Multi-block sloper and terrain shaper (1x1 to 7x7).
    - Cycles slopes with dedicated presets that mirror natural world-generation curves.
4. Tile & Wall FAA-bricators (V1 & V2)
    - Mass-placement tools for blocks and walls across 1x1, 3x3, and 5x5 areas.
    - Full compatibility with Block Swap and auto-paint settings.
5. Swiss Army Painting Tools (Brush, Roller, Scraper)
    - Multi-tile painting and scraping across 1x1, 3x3, and 5x5 sizes.
    - Available across V1 and V2 progression tiers.
6. XL Bottomless Buckets & Absorbent Sponges
    - Area placement and liquid drainage in 1x1, 3x3, and 5x5 sweeps.
    - Available for Water, Lava, Honey, and Shimmer.
    - Includes the XL Ultra Absorbent Sponge and XL Bottomless Omni-Bucket (cycles all 4 liquids).
7. Improved Actuation Rod
    - Actuate blocks across 1x1, 3x3, 5x5, and 7x7 areas using actuators.
    - Secondary 1x1 mode allows actuator-free toggling.
8. The Proper Design (Arena Builder)
    - Rapidly builds arena scaffoldings and platforms (Left, Center, and Right variants).
    - Places nearby buff stations and furniture directly from your inventory.
    - Preserves infinite and non-consumable modded stations (e.g., Fargo's Omni Station).

HOW TO OBTAIN:
All base tools are sold directly by the Goblin Tinkerer as your world progresses:
    - Available Immediately: Automatic Auto-Mattock V1, Another Auto-Hammer V1, Swiss Army Brush V1, Tile FAA-bricator V1, Wall FAA-bricator V1, Tile Pounder, and The Proper Design.
    - Post-Skeletron: Improved Actuation Rod.
    - 5 Angler Quests: XL Bottomless Water Bucket & XL Super Absorbent Sponge.
    - Post-Queen Bee: XL Bottomless Honey Bucket & XL Honey Absorbent Sponge.
    - Hardmode: XL Bottomless Lava Bucket & XL Lava Absorbent Sponge.
    - Post-Moon Lord: XL Bottomless Shimmer Bucket.
    - Higher tiers (V2 variants, Omni-Bucket, Ultra Sponge) can be upgraded and crafted as you progress.
